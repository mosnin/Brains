How to Play

Speed Mental Math is a brain speed training game designed to improve your mental arithmetic. Each level presents 10 math problems that you must solve before time runs out. In early levels, select from four multiple-choice answers. As you progress, you will type your answers directly. Score at least 80% to advance to the next level.

Benefits of Mental Math Training

Regular mental math practice strengthens working memory, improves processing speed, and builds numerical fluency. Research shows that brain speed training games like mental arithmetic exercises can enhance cognitive performance across many areas of daily life, from financial calculations to better decision-making under pressure. By training with progressively harder problems, you build lasting mental agility.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Speed Mental Math improve brain speed?

Speed Mental Math trains your brain to process numerical information faster through timed practice. Each level progressively increases difficulty, strengthening neural pathways associated with arithmetic processing, working memory, and quick decision-making.

What math skills are covered in the 30 levels?

The game covers addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, mixed operations, multi-step expressions, squares and square roots, percentages, fractions, estimation, and chain operations. Difficulty scales from single-digit arithmetic to complex multi-operation problems.

How is my score calculated?

Each level presents 10 problems. Your score is the percentage of correct answers (correct answers divided by 10, times 100). You need at least 80% (8 out of 10 correct) to pass a level and unlock the next one.
