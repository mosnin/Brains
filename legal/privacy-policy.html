Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BrainForge ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website and play our free brain training games.

By using BrainForge, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies:

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, clicks, and navigation paths
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, and device type
  • IP Address: Your IP address may be collected by third-party analytics services (anonymized where possible)

2.2 Locally Stored Data

We use your browser's localStorage to save your game progress, scores, and preferences. This data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. This includes:

  • Game progress and highest levels achieved
  • Scores and star ratings for each level
  • Sound preferences and settings
  • Cookie consent preferences

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address. No account creation is required to use our services.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide and maintain our brain training games
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our games and website
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Ensure the technical functionality and security of our website

4. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use our site, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to collect and analyze usage data. Google Analytics uses cookies to track and report website traffic. This data is shared with Google. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies. For detailed information about the cookies we use and your choices, please see our Cookie Policy.

7. Data Retention

Game progress data stored in localStorage persists until you clear your browser data or manually remove it. We do not maintain any server-side user data. Third-party analytics and advertising data is retained according to the respective third party's data retention policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: You can access your game data through your browser's developer tools (localStorage)
  • Deletion: You can delete all your local data by clearing your browser's storage
  • Opt-out: You can opt out of personalized advertising and analytics tracking
  • GDPR Rights: If you are in the EU/EEA, you have additional rights under the GDPR including the right to access, rectification, erasure, and data portability
  • CCPA Rights: If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what data is collected and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@brainforge.app
