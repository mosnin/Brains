Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide information to website owners, and enhance the user experience. We also use browser localStorage, which serves a similar purpose but stores data locally on your device without transmitting it to any server.

2. How We Use Cookies and Local Storage

BrainForge uses cookies and localStorage for the following purposes:

Name/Type Purpose Duration Category
brainforge_progress Stores your game progress, scores, and level completions Persistent (until cleared) Essential
brainforge_settings Stores your preferences (sound on/off) Persistent (until cleared) Essential
brainforge_cookies_accepted Records your cookie consent preference Persistent (until cleared) Essential
_ga, _gid Google Analytics - tracks page views and user behavior anonymously Up to 2 years Analytics
_gat Google Analytics - used to throttle request rate 1 minute Analytics
__gads, __gpi Google AdSense - serves and measures advertising Up to 13 months Advertising
DSID, IDE Google DoubleClick - ad targeting and measurement Up to 2 years Advertising
NID, CONSENT Google - stores user preferences and consent Up to 6 months Functional

3. Essential Cookies and Local Storage

Essential cookies and localStorage items are necessary for the basic functionality of our website. They enable features such as saving your game progress and remembering your preferences. Without these, the Website would not function properly. These do not require your consent as they are strictly necessary.

4. Analytics Cookies

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. These cookies help us measure the number of visitors, identify which pages are most popular, and understand how users navigate our site. All analytics data is anonymized and aggregated. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. These cookies enable Google to:

  • Serve ads based on your interests and past visits
  • Limit the number of times you see a particular ad
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Here is how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website. In particular, disabling localStorage will prevent us from saving your game progress.

7. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner will appear. You can choose to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Essential cookies (localStorage for game progress) will always be active as they are necessary for the website to function. If you decline, advertising and analytics cookies will not be loaded.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at: privacy@brainforge.app
